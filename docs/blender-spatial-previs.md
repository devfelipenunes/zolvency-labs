# Spatial typography Blender previs

Real geometry comes from decoded, visible facility roots captured by the opt-in diagnostics hook, including mesh world transforms and the current camera. No generated stand-in assets. Materials are deliberately simplified to untextured base color, emission, roughness and metalness; transmissive glass groups are omitted. Camera-relative lights are a previs approximation. Not a final-material screenshot or a production site change.

Three studies: office chair reveal + desk surface (if detected by ray sampling, otherwise console vertical plane); quantum axis vs off-axis; Stellar rack-aligned vs aisle plane. Two anchored positions per room, three keyframes per position (18 renders), real depth/occlusion, no camera-parented text. Each candidate saves its own editable Blender file. Camera path is small rotation about actual measured scene hit, with straight-line collision guard; not a proof of whole-path clearance. Text bounding-box visibility is an approximate BVH sample, not per-letter readability. Final body remains native HTML for accessible reading. Compare full-size renders and scrub keyframes; reject unreadable or collision-prone results. If export/install/render fails, report the failure rather than simulating Blender.

Research: Blender camera and depth of field (https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cameras.html), glTF (https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/scene_gltf2.html); Three.js text (https://threejs.org/manual/pages/creating-text.html), material depth/clipping (https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Material.html), CSS3DRenderer limitations (https://threejs.org/docs/pages/CSS3DRenderer.html). Export decoded meshes rather than relying on Blender support of meshopt/KTX2 compressed assets. Keep physical glyph planes for genuine occlusion; use DOM for long copy; CSS3D does not directly share WebGL depth. No public default or #131 change.
