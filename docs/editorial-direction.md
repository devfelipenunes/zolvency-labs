# Editorial roadmap — R34

This opt-in branch is based on the consolidated #131 candidate. Control copy, rendering defaults and existing production are unchanged. A/B/C are proposals, not a launch decision.

## Four parallel workstreams
- E01/E02/E03: manifesto, precision, exploration; seven complete EN chapters each. One semantic source for the selected HTML and spatial text. Specific blockchain/Stellar applications, separate quantum/post-quantum fields, AI and quantum research rather than product claims. Preserve original control for comparison.
- E04/E05/E08: local Arial/Helvetica and system-ui audition, no external font fetch. Larger headline hierarchy, reduced label decoration, feathered contrast surface, distinct scene placements, portrait and short landscape. Typography selection is provisional, not a comprehensive custom typeface audit.
- E06/E07: preserve existing camera-progress beats and mask, redistribute native scroll distance so full opacity has approximately 0.9–1.2 viewport-height budgets according to copy length. Both scroll mapper and motion controller use same edition table. No timers, forced scrolling or scroll lock. Fast scrolling can still skip copy; distance is not guaranteed reading time. Animate opacity without additional translation for new editions. Evaluate increased overall scroll distance and actual camera motion.
- E09/E10/E11: control + three editions × seven actual rendered holds × two viewports = 56 captures, same camera/config. Fixed-pose comparison plus native forward/reverse scroll with selected manifesto. Check bounds, opacity, font size, conservative contrast, exact copy, fallback, reduced motion and mechanics through existing integrated tests. Native smoke on manifesto only; other editions require native runs if selected. Need inspect screenshots at full resolution, actual device and reader comprehension before promotion.

## Human comprehension protocol (not yet conducted)
Recruit 5–8 readers including non-specialists and English speakers; randomize edition order across participants, ask them to explain after one visit: what the company builds now, what role Stellar has, which areas are research, and what makes the company distinctive. Observe rereads/skipped acts, scroll reversals, legibility and scene obstruction. Record exact viewport, browser, completion and comments; compare against control. Avoid declaring better comprehension based on automated bounds checks alone. Prefer a candidate only if it preserves clarity and visual quality, has no scroll regressions, and passes hardware review. No fabricated participants or scores.
