# V0.16 — Zolvency Facility
A conceptual technological facility, not a depiction of real premises. One sourced Tevy hangar as the continuous architecture. Nine external CC0 models: hangar, straight corridor, bridge, corridor door, server room, hologram projector, Poly Haven PCB (Benny Weimer), GPU and cryostat. All staged from pinned SHA-256 locks. The active renderer uses no authored geometric models; prior procedural catalog is retained in source history and legacy tests but excluded from the production stage. Corner, junction, control room downloaded for audition, not placed. Original hangar roof/walls, blast door and crane preserved. Bridge front wall hidden to join two actual corridor modules, door leaves animated on scroll. Server room front glass and door preserved; camera approaches its threshold, not a claim of full traversal. The tour remains inside one 20 x 30 m building at 1.8–2.4 m camera height. A deliberate turn returns from the intelligence wing to the central hall. Real lighting, source materials, native scroll, fixed semantic HTML and reduced-motion fallback; no sound yet. No claim of physical real-world building, proof protocol implementation, physical GPU FPS or continuous collision guarantee. Screenshots and sampled mesh clearance required before release.
