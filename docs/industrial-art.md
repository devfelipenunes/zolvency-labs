# V0.12 industrial art library

52 catalog entries are parametric variants, not 52 hand-sculpted hero models. Families: 5 ledger, 5 crypto, 4 processor, 10 electronics, 8 quantum accessories, 5 architectural frames, 15 fragments. Stellar and cryostat hero collections are additional. Shared authored blueprints, no third-party assets. The website uses native procedural geometry, with indexed GLB replacement for the two complex static heroes; Blender library is independently editable. Preserve V0.11 camera path; model envelopes remain constrained by previous passage. Seven physical material finishes; smoked glass is an opaque glossy approximation for web performance, not refractive transmission. Microtexture uses tiny generated normal/roughness maps. Local emissive sprites are a low-cost selective glow, not full-screen bloom. Depth-of-field and AO intentionally deferred until measured. GPU physical tests, artistic approval and launch permission remain open.
