Opt-in &travel=responsive. Keep baseline and current candidate controls unchanged. Integrate elapsed time up to 250ms, normal step 0.35 vh and sustained delayed cadence up to 0.70 vh, desired speed capped 7.9 vh/s, acceleration command 24 vh/s², jerk command 180 vh/s³; reversal/endpoint resets take priority. Physical camera jerk is not guaranteed. Three-way benchmark and actual pacing invariants; compare settlement, first motion, frame gaps and target error. Larger recovery steps can introduce jumps; no release promotion before motion review.
